The UrbanNest Journey

Rooted in Chennai, Guided by Human-Centric Design

UrbanNest Interiors was founded in 2018 by principal architect Ananya Iyer on a simple, enduring conviction: a home should never feel like a sterile showroom, but rather a seamless physical extension of the people who inhabit it.

Over the past 8 years, our studio has expanded into a tight-knit family of 14 seasoned architects, structural visualizers, material specialists, and on-site craftsmen. We understand the unique architectural demands of Chennai living—from maximizing cross-ventilation against coastal humidity to sourcing durable, termite-proof hardwood and low-VOC finishes that endure for generations.

Whether we are designing a compact 2BHK in Anna Nagar or an expansive contemporary villa along the East Coast Road, our priority remains unchanged: timeless aesthetics anchored by flawless engineering.

Meet Our Leadership Team

Supported by our broader studio team of 14 architects, civil engineers, and artisan carpenters, our leadership team directs every design with uncompromising precision.

Ananya Iyer

Founder & Principal Designer

With a Master's in Architecture from CEG Guindy, Ananya brings over 12 years of residential design expertise, specializing in organic minimalism and climate-responsive spatial planning.

Rohit Menon

Head of Projects

Civil engineer with a decade of on-site execution rigor. Rohit manages project timelines, procurement pipelines, and weekly milestone audits with zero tolerance for cost overruns.

Sneha Pillai

Senior 3D Visualizer

Master of photorealistic CGI and material fidelity. Sneha bridges conceptual client discussions into vivid 4K walkthroughs, ensuring every grain of wood and beam of light is perfected before site execution.

Vijay Kumar

Site Operations Lead

Overseeing our master carpentry and on-site MEP teams across Chennai, Vijay ensures stringent safety, seamless joinery tolerances, and pristine handover readiness.
