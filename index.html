Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your interior design journey with UrbanNest Interiors.

Our initial 30-minute design consultation is completely free of charge. You can meet our lead architects at our Anna Nagar design studio or schedule a video call. We discuss your floor plans, aesthetic tastes, budget parameters, and timeline requirements.
A complete turnkey 3BHK interior project typically takes 45 to 60 business days from the final design approval. We maintain an ironclad schedule backed by weekly video updates and a penalty clause for unexcused delays.
