Our Proven Method

The Four-Step Journey to Your New Space

A transparent, predictable process engineered to eliminate surprises and keep you in total control at every phase.

01

CONSULT

Free 30-Minute Consultation: Meet our principal architects at our Anna Nagar studio or online. We review your floor plans, lifestyle requirements, aesthetic tastes, and budget parameters.

02

DESIGN

Layout, 3D Views & Materials: We develop comprehensive 2D spatial layouts, realistic 4K 3D views, and guide you through tactile material swatches (veneers, quartz, laminates, fabrics).

03

BUILD

Site Execution with Weekly Updates: Precision fabrication in our controlled carpentry facility followed by on-site civil and electrical assembly. You receive weekly video logs and milestone audits.

04

HANDOVER

Inspection & 1-Year Support: Rigorous 120-point quality audit, professional deep cleaning, formal keys handover, and activation of our 1-year comprehensive service warranty.
